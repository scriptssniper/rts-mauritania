# Character Profiles — Mauritania Resistance RTS
### Game-ready hero/NPC profiles, anchored to RESEARCH_campaign_history.md (2026-11-01)
Legend: **[H]** = playable hero unit · **[N]** = NPC/story figure. Quotes sourced from research doc; abilities are design proposals.

## FRENCH SIDE

### 1. Xavier Coppolani — "Le Charmeur" [H→N] (1866–1905)
- **Role:** Diplomat-general. Act I protagonist.
- **Gameplay:** No combat stats. Aura: *Parole, pas fusil* — converts neutral tribes to protectorate treaties without fighting. Builds borj network. Economy: subsidy/tribute mechanics.
- **Personality:** Corsican Arabist, hypnotic speaker, genuine admiration for Islam — manipulates with sincerity. "In Muslim lands, the word religion replaces the word patrie."
- **Arc:** Rise 1898→1904 → assassinated 12 May 1905 (scripted). Death = campaign midpoint trigger, French AI doctrine flips from diplomacy to columns.
- **Death scene assets:** Palmery at Tidjikja, night, milk delivery. Last words: *"Ces misérables m'ont tué — ils ne me méritent pas."* Tomb epitaph (Arabic): "the friend of the Muslims."
- **Design note:** Playable in missions 1–4. His death mission plays from the RESISTANCE side (see Sidi Seghir).

### 2. Col. Henri Gouraud — The Column [H] (1867–1946)
- **Role:** Act II French protagonist. Mechanized-war doctrine made flesh.
- **Gameplay:** Buffs camel columns (march speed, water discipline = slower thirst drain). Ability *Contre-rezzou* — mirrors resistance raid tactics. Unlocks méhariste companies.
- **Personality:** Cold professional, Lyautey protégé. Doctrine quote (load screen): "Not a matter of conquering the Adrar for permanent occupation… but of organizing a police column to purge the country."
- **Arc:** Arrives 1907 after three years of French paralysis. Hamdoun pass 8 Jan 1909 → Atar 9 Jan 1909. Leaves end-1909 (promoted — future Gallipoli/Levant commander, governor of Paris).
- **Visual:** Képi, one-armed gesture lore optional — NOT in sources, skip unless verified.

### 3. Capt. Frèrejean — The Post-Builder [N] (fl. 1904–1909)
- **Role:** French "builder" hero; the borj lattice personified. Founded Nouakchott (1904), led 1909 reinforcements.
- **Gameplay hook:** Mission type "link the forts" — escort + construct chain Tagant→Adrar.

### 4. Lt. Etiévent — The Avenger [N]
- **Role:** Kills Coppolani's assassin with two revolver shots, pins corpse with own saber. Single cinematic cameo + optional duel scenario vs Sidi Seghir.

## RESISTANCE SIDE

### 5. Cheikh Ma El Aïnin — The Saint of Smara [H] (1831–1910)
- **Role:** Central resistance figure, campaign co-protagonist. Scholar (~140 books), Qadiriyya Fadiliyya head, sultan's delegate.
- **Gameplay:** Not a combat unit — a **living wonder**. Aura: *Jihad* — rallies warrior tribes, converts neutral camps, raises levies. Ability *Baraka* — morale immunity (units never rout near him). If he dies on the field = faction-wide collapse timer.
- **Personality:** Iron-willed ascetic, builder (Smara library, arms store), diplomat to sultans and (later) Germany. Self-proclaimed Mahdi 1909 — arc tilts tragic: charisma outlives armies.
- **Arc:** Proclaims jihad 1904 → Smara falls 1908–09 → marches 5,000–6,000 men to raise Morocco → broken at Tadla 23 Jun 1910 → dies Tiznit 23 Oct 1910, aged 79, "undefeated on Mauritanian soil."
- **Visual:** White turban, green banner, manuscript saddle-bags. Literary canon: Le Clézio's *Désert*.
- **Supply lane:** Sultan's steamship *Turki* (German captain Karow) → Tarfaya → guns south. WWI: German submarine 1916. Resource mechanic, historically real.

### 6. Sidi Seghir Ould Moulay Zein — The Dream-Vow [H, one mission] (d. 1905)
- **Role:** Coppolani's assassin. Moqadem of Cheikh Elghazwany; acted on a dream-prophecy.
- **Gameplay:** Stealth mission "The Milk Delivery" — 27 Tenaki warriors, 5-minute window (21h25–21h30), palmery at night. Asymmetric stealth: kill Coppolani, extract. Historical outcome: 8 of 20 attackers die; he is shot by Etiévent.
- **Design note:** The mission France plays in reverse later (defense of the palmery as alt-history? No — keep history fixed: French side experiences it as scripted loss that triggers Act II).

### 7. Ahmed al-Hiba — The Blue Sultan [H] (c.1876–1919)
- **Role:** Eldest son. Act III opening protagonist.
- **Gameplay:** Highest levy multiplier — *Sultan's Banner* raises mass armies fast (10,000 at Marrakech) but poor unit quality/coordination.
- **Arc:** Enters Marrakech 14 Aug 1912, proclaimed Sultan → Mangin's spahis + tirailleurs destroy army at Sidi Bou Othmane 6 Sep 1912 (~2,000 dead). Exile, dies Kerdous 23 Jun 1919. Tragedy: the one resistance leader who took a city — and lost an empire in three weeks.

### 8. Agdaf — The Vengeance [H] (fl. 1913)
- **Role:** Son of Ma El Aïnin. Act III "dark middle."
- **Gameplay:** *Reprisal* ability — after Smara burns (1913 scripted event), his raids get critical-hit bonus vs posts. Mission chain: Liboirat (60 French dead), Tagliat (22+37), Bou-Telis (23 dead, 2 survivors).
- **Design note:** These are massacre scenarios — handle tone: name the historical dead, no gore-reward loop. Objective framing = "break the occupation's grip," not kill-count.

### 9. Merebbi Rebbu — The Remnant [N] (d. after 1919)
- **Role:** Successor 1919; holds Saguia el-Hamra rump. Late-campaign quest-giver for Reguibat raids (Port-Étienne 1924/1927, grand rezzou 1934).

### 10. Emir Sidi Ahmed Ould Ahmed Ayda — The Puppet [N] (r. 1909–1932)
- **Role:** The tragedy arc. Adrar emir.
- **Arc:** Installed 1909 (anti-French) → captured Tichfit 1910 [CONFLICT: 1909] → **restored 1913** as hollow French puppet → defects to dissidence 1931 → dies in the desert 1932. Emirate (c.1740–1932) ends with him.
- **Gameplay:** Diplomacy-system centerpiece — his allegiance meter swings campaign rules for the whole Adrar region.

### 11. Cheikh Saad Bouh — The Other Brother [N] (1848–1917)
- **Role:** The war's conscience. Pro-French pacifist marabout, Ma El Aïnin's brother — same father, same brotherhood, opposite war.
- **Personality:** Mediator (saved explorer Soleillet 1880), 664 consecrated sheikhs, vast Senegal network.
- **Arc beat:** Writes the *Nasîha* (1906) — theological refutation of jihad, addressed to his own brother: "He who takes arms departs from virtue." Dies 1917.
- **Design note:** Campaign narrator candidate — the internal Mauritanian voice that lets the game be about civil war, not just France-vs-Maure. His vs Ma El Aïnin's letters as between-mission text.

### 12. Cheikh Sidiya Baba — The Kingmaker [N]
- **Role:** Qadiriyya-Sidiyya head, Boutilimit. Coppolani's principal Mauritanian ally; Trarza/Brakna/Tagant submissions flow through him. The "peaceful penetration" tech tree in human form.

## TRIBE UNITS (flavor anchors, not heroes)
- **Rguibat (Reguibat)** — irreconcilables. Sacked Tindouf 1896 (1,200 men, 7-day battle); hit Port-Étienne 1924+1927; deserted en masse 1932; final 6,000-km grand rezzou 1934. Elite raider tree.
- **Tenaki** — Coppolani's assassins' tribe.
- **Ouled Delim** — méharistes who deserted 1932 with weapons (mission: the defection).
- **Tekna, Ouled Ghar, Kounta (Hodh)** — regional levies.

## FRENCH UNITS (flavor anchors)
- **Tirailleurs sénégalais** — backbone; camel-mounted; battle honors "Mauritanie 1902", "Mauritanie 1904–1913". NO Legion (sources explicit), artillery = few mountain pieces only.
