# THE UNDEFEATED SOIL — Soundtrack Prompts (Suno / Udio)
Usage Suno: paste STYLE block into "Style of Music", STRUCTURE block into Lyrics (custom mode), toggle Instrumental ON.
Usage Udio: STYLE into prompt, tags as words, Instrumental mode.
Rule: never use real artist names (blocked + bad practice). Obsolete-instrument names are glossed — models know the gloss, not the name.

## 1. Main Theme — "Undefeated Soil" (menu)
STYLE:
West African orchestral film score, Mauritanian Hassaniya traditional fusion, male griot vocals chanting in distant reverb, Moorish lute (tidinit, like a desert oud), ardin harp, tbal frame drums building slowly, cinematic desert epic, 70 bpm, minor key (D minor), long reverb, wide stereo, dusty analog warmth, no pop drums, no synth
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – solo Moorish lute, sparse, wind ambience]
[Verse – harp arpeggios join, distant chant]
[Build – frame drums enter, low strings swell]
[Climax – full orchestra + chant, regal and mournful]
[Outro – decay to wind and solo lute]

## 2. Resistance Theme — "Rezzou" (battle, resistance side)
STYLE:
driving Mauritanian desert battle music, galloping tbal frame drum pattern 130 bpm, tidinit lute ostinato, ardin harp interjections, raw male ululation echoes in distance, hand percussion layers, modal Phrygian, urgent and joyful-wild, live room feel, no guitars, no electronic
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – distant hoofbeats, single drum pulse]
[Main riff – lute ostinato + gallop drums]
[Break – ululation, drums drop to heartbeat]
[Return – full percussion stack, hand claps]
[Outro – drums cut, hoofbeats recede]

## 3. French Theme — "The Column" (French side, slow menace)
STYLE:
colonial-era military march meets Saharan dread, muted bugle calls, field snare with loose rudiments, camel bells and creaking leather, brass choir distant across dunes, 85 bpm, A minor, dusty and oppressive, sparse, field-recording texture, no modern drums, no choir
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – wind, camel bells, boots on gravel]
[March theme – snare + bugle unison]
[Middle – brass swells, snare falls to rolls]
[Ending – bugle alone, echoed, unresolved]

## 4. Water Clock — "Thirst" (attrition/ambient, any map)
STYLE:
minimal desert ambient, solo ney flute phrases with long silence between, single low drum heartbeat every 4 bars, dry heat shimmer drone, no melody resolution, 50 bpm, extremely sparse, meditative dread, no percussion groove, no bass
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – wind drone 8 bars]
[Phrase – ney alone]
[Silence + heartbeat]
[Phrase – ney, lower, weaker]
[Outro – drone thins to nothing]

## 5. The Poets' War — "Kāf" (poems screen / zawiya)
STYLE:
traditional Hassaniya court music, ardin harp solo with female vocal melisma, tidinit lute reply, intimate small-room recording, gentle tbal pulse, 95 bpm, Dorian mode, scholarly and tender, museum-quiet dynamic, no drums buildup, no orchestra
STRUCTURE:
[Verse – harp + female vocal call]
[Reply – lute answers]
[Verse 2 – voices overlap softly]
[Close – harp alone, unresolved]

## 6. Smara — "The Unfinished City" (Wonder objective / Smara missions)
STYLE:
sacred desert lament, male chant with cathedral reverb over ruins, bowed drone strings, faint wind and brick dust, tidinit lute fragments dissolving, 60 bpm, Phrygian dominant, monumental sorrow, no rhythm section at all
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – wind through empty windows]
[Chant – solo voice, immense reverb]
[Drone – strings under chant]
[Fragment – lute plays half a phrase, stops]
[Final – chant fades mid-word]

## 7. Act III — "Pacification" (1934 endgame)
STYLE:
post-war elegiac orchestral, Maurice Jarre-style desert strings without naming, solo trumpet lament over wide empty reverb, tbal drum funeral pattern once, 65 bpm, exhausted major-minor shifts, cinema scope, no chant, no percussion groove
STRUCTURE:
[Intro – low strings, horizon-wide]
[Lament – solo trumpet theme]
[Memory – faint frame drum, 2 bars, gone]
[End – strings hold, no resolution, wind]
